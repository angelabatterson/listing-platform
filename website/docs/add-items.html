Every item in Relistic is one master record: title, description, price, quantity, condition and photos. You tick the stores it should appear on, and Relistic keeps all of those listings matched to it.

Add an item

  1. Click Inventory in the left menu.
  2. Click Add item in the top right.
  3. Fill in the Title, Description, Condition, Brand, Price (USD) and Quantity. The SKU is filled in for you, but you can change it.
  4. Optional: type What you paid. Only you see it; the dashboard uses it to work out your profit.
  5. Optional: add Tags, your own groups separated by commas, such as Bin 4, Women's denim.
  6. Under Where to list it, tick each store this item should appear on.
  7. Set Status to Active (list on the stores ticked below). Leave it on Draft if you're not ready; drafts aren't listed anywhere.
  8. Click Save. Relistic posts it to each ticked store and shows the result on the item.

Photos

The quickest way to add photos is a photo batch, even for a single item. You can also paste links to photos that are already online under Photo links, one per line. The first photo is the main one.

Store-specific details

Some stores need extra details. Relistic asks for them on the item when that store is ticked:

  • eBay: a category and its item specifics. Use the category finder on the item page, and click to fill the specifics eBay requires with AI.
  • Etsy: a category and When was it made?
  • TikTok Shop: a category is optional; if you leave it empty, Relistic uses the one TikTok suggests for the title.

The item page can also show prices of similar items currently for sale on eBay, to help you price. These are asking prices, not sold prices.

A different price or title on one store

  1. On the Inventory page, find the item and click its store (for example eBay) in the Stores column.
  2. In the pop-up, change the title, description, condition, price or photos for that store only.
  3. Click Save.

Anything you leave empty uses the item's main details.

Edit an item

Click the item on the Inventory page, change anything, and click Save. Relistic updates every store it's listed on. If a store had a problem, click Sync now to try again.

Remove an item

On the item page, click Archive and end listings. The item ends on every store and moves to the Archived tab, so your history and profit numbers stay correct.

Find items fast

On the Inventory page, use the tabs (All, Listed, Drafts, Sold, Archived), search by title or SKU, and filter by store, status or tag.
