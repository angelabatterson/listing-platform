List many items at once

Bulk crosslist puts a group of items on more stores in one go, for example every item you imported from eBay onto Etsy and Poshmark.

  1. Click Inventory in the left menu.
  2. Tick the box next to each item you want to list. To pick every item on the page, tick the box at the top of the list.
  3. Click Crosslist in the dark bar that opens at the bottom of the screen (it shows how many are selected, for example 12 items selected). A pop-up opens with three steps.

Step 1: Stores and titles

  1. Under Which stores?, tick the stores to add the items to.
  2. Under Titles, choose Shorten long titles with AI to fit each store or Keep titles as they are. Only titles over a store's limit change, and only on that store. If you keep them, a store may turn down a title that's too long.
  3. Click Next.

Step 2: Prices

For each store, pick how its price is set from the item's main price:

  • Price rule: uses the store's price rule (shown only when the store has one; see below).
  • Same as main price.
  • Raise by % or Lower by %, then type the percent.

The example shows what one of your items will cost on each store. Click Next.

Step 3: Drafts or publish

Under When should they go up?:

  • Publish now posts them to the stores you picked right away. Drafts among them are also posted to the other stores already ticked on them.
  • Save as drafts ticks the stores, with your prices and titles, on each draft so you can check them and post later. This only works for items that are still drafts; already-listed items are skipped.

Click Crosslist (or Save … as drafts) and keep the pop-up open while it works. When it's done, it says how many were listed and lists any item that needs a fix, such as a missing category, so you can open it and finish it. Click Back to change an earlier step, or Cancel to stop before starting.

Use the tabs and filters first to narrow the list, for example the Listed tab with Any store set to eBay. See Find and organize your inventory.

Price rules per store

Stores charge different fees, so you may want different prices on each. A price rule changes an item's price automatically on one store, without typing a price per item.

  1. Click your name at the bottom of the left menu, then Stores.
  2. Scroll down to Price rules.
  3. Next to a store, type a percent under Raise or lower by. Use a minus sign to lower, for example -5.
  4. Under Rounding, choose whether prices end in .99, are rounded to whole dollars, or are left as they are.
  5. Check the example under A $40.00 item becomes.
  6. Click Save price rules.

The rule is used wherever you haven't typed a price for that store on the item, and it's offered as Price rule in step 2 of Crosslist. A price you type in Price on this store always wins. The Inventory page and the Chrome extension show the price after the rule.
