Relistic keeps one list of everything you sell. You add an item once, tick the stores it should appear on, and Relistic posts it to each of them. When it sells on one store, Relistic takes it down (or lowers the stock) everywhere else.

1. Create your account

  1. Go to app.getrelistic.com/signup.
  2. Type your name, your business name, your email address and a password.
  3. Click Create account. Your 14-day free trial starts now. You don't need a card.

2. Find your way around

The menu on the left has everything you use day to day:

  • Dashboard: your sales, fees and profit.
  • CRM: the people who bought from you, with your notes.
  • Inventory: every item, and which stores it's on.
  • Photo batches: turn a pile of photos into draft listings with AI.
  • Automations: eBay offers and relisting.
  • Activity: a log of everything Relistic did, and any problems.

Click your name at the bottom of the menu for Stores, Shipping, Account settings and Billing. On a phone, tap the menu button at the top first.

3. Connect a store

Click your name, then Stores, and connect the stores you sell on. Each one has its own short guide in Connect your stores. For Poshmark, Depop, Mercari, Grailed, Facebook Marketplace and Whatnot, see The Chrome extension.

4. Add your items

There are three ways, and you can mix them:

  • Import your current eBay or Etsy listings, so they start syncing straight away.
  • Upload photos in a photo batch and let the AI write the listings.
  • Type an item in yourself.

5. Sell

That's it. When something sells, Relistic updates your other stores within minutes and puts the order on the Shipping page. Read How syncing works to see exactly what happens.
