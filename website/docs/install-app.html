Relistic works in any web browser, and you can add it to your phone's home screen or your computer so it opens with one tap, full screen, like an app. There's nothing to download from an app store.

Log in at app.getrelistic.com first, in the browser you'll use. You stay logged in when you open Relistic from the icon.

iPhone or iPad, with Safari

  1. Open Safari and go to app.getrelistic.com.
  2. Tap the ⋯ button in the bottom right corner.
  3. Tap Share.
  4. Tap View More.
  5. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  6. Leave the name as Relistic and tap Add.

Don't see ⋯? On older iPhones, tap the Share button instead (a square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen), then scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

iPhone or iPad, with Chrome

  1. Open Chrome and go to app.getrelistic.com.
  2. Tap the Share icon (a square with an arrow pointing up) at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

Android, with Chrome

  1. Open Chrome and go to app.getrelistic.com.
  2. Tap the ⋮ (three dots) in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (on some phones it says Install app).
  4. Tap Install or Add. If your phone asks, tap Add again to place the icon.

Windows or Mac computer, with Chrome or Edge

  1. Go to app.getrelistic.com.
  2. Look at the right end of the address bar for a small install icon (a screen with a down arrow) and click it. No icon? In Chrome, click ⋮, then Cast, save, and share, then Install page as app. In Edge, click ⋯, then Apps, then Install this site as an app.
  3. Click Install.

Relistic opens in its own window, and you'll find it in your Start menu (Windows) or Applications folder and Launchpad (Mac).

Mac, with Safari

  1. Go to app.getrelistic.com.
  2. In the menu bar at the top of the screen, click File, then Add to Dock.
  3. Click Add.

Good to know

  • The icon always opens the latest version of Relistic. There's nothing to update.
  • The Chrome extension for Poshmark, Depop and the others still needs Chrome on a computer.
  • To remove the icon, press and hold it on your phone and choose Remove or Delete, like any app.
