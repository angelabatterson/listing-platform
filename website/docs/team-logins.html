On the Pro and Business plans you can give helpers, like an assistant or a family member who lists for you, their own login to your Relistic account. Each helper sees only the pages you choose, and never your store connections or billing.

Invite a helper

  1. Click your name at the bottom of the left menu, then Team.
  2. Under Invite a helper, type their email address in Their email.
  3. Under Pages they can use, tick each page they need. You can choose from: Dashboard, CRM, Inventory and the Chrome extension, Sales, Photo batches, Automations, Activity and Shipping labels.
  4. Click Make invite link.
  5. Click Copy, then paste the link into a text or email to your helper. Relistic doesn't send it for you.

The link works once, for 7 days. Send it only to the person you're inviting.

What your helper does

  1. Open the invite link.
  2. Type their name and choose a password (10 or more characters).
  3. Click Join the team.

From then on, they log in at app.getrelistic.com with their email and password. Their menu shows only the pages you ticked.

Change or remove a helper

  • Change their pages: on the Team page, under Helpers, tick or untick pages and click Save pages.
  • Remove them: click Remove helper and confirm. They're logged out straight away and can't log in again. Anything they listed or changed stays.
  • Invite not used yet: under Waiting to join, click New link to make a fresh link (the old one stops working), or Cancel invite.

What helpers can't do

Helpers never see the Stores, Billing or Team pages, and can't close the account. Only you, the account owner, can connect stores, change the plan or manage the team.
