About Relistic

We build tools that give resellers their time back.

Why we made Relistic

Selling secondhand means selling everywhere. A good find might sell on eBay, Etsy, Poshmark or TikTok Shop, so most resellers list the same item on several sites. That means typing it in again and again, and then racing to take it down everywhere else when it sells, before someone buys something that's already gone.

Relistic started when a reseller we work with asked for a simpler way to do this. It keeps one inventory for your whole business. You write each item once, choose the stores it belongs on, and Relistic keeps every listing in step: same stock, the prices you set, and gone from every store as soon as it sells.

What Relistic does

  • Crosslisting: posts each item to the stores you tick, with a different price or title per store if you want.
  • Inventory sync: when something sells on one store, it lowers the stock or ends the listing on all the others.
  • AI listing assistant: turns a pile of photos into draft listings for you to check and post.
  • Selling tools: shipping labels, eBay offer and relist automations, a profit dashboard and a buyer CRM.

How we connect to your stores

Relistic uses each marketplace's official developer program wherever one is available, including eBay, Etsy, Shopify and TikTok Shop. You sign in on the marketplace's own page and approve access, and you can disconnect at any time. Relistic never sees your marketplace passwords, and the access keys stores give us are stored encrypted.

For stores that don't offer a way for other apps to post, such as Poshmark, Depop and Mercari, the Relistic Chrome extension fills in the listing form in your own browser and you press Post yourself.

Your data

Your listings, sales and buyer details are yours. We use them only to run Relistic for you, never for advertising, and we never sell them. You can close your account at any time, which deletes everything in it. Read the full privacy policy.
