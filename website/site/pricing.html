Questions

Do I need a card to start the trial?

No. Sign up and use Relistic free for 14 days. Pick a plan on the Billing page whenever you're ready; any trial days you have left are kept.

What counts as an active listing?

An item set to Active in your inventory, however many stores it's on. One item listed on six stores counts once.

What's an AI draft?

One item the AI listing assistant writes for you from your photos: title, description, condition, details and a suggested price.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, any time, from Billing in the menu under your name. Changes and cancellations are handled on Stripe's secure pages.

What happens if my plan ends?

Your items and history are kept, and sales keep syncing so nothing oversells. You just can't post, edit or use the AI until you pick a plan again.
